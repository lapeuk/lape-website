YOUR FUTURE IN THE UK

Not just how to visit London.
How to build a future here.

A key part of the LAPE experience is helping young people understand what studying and building a future in the UK can actually look like.

Participants will receive guidance about British colleges and universities, study pathways, applications and the practical steps involved in education and life in the UK.

We will also visit the University of Greenwich, giving students the opportunity to experience a London university environment from the inside.
