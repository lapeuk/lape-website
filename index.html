Your future

What if London became more than a trip?

Alongside the journey, participants get a real look at studying in the UK — university pathways, admissions, funding options, student life, and what it's actually like to live and learn in London.

An educational consultation is included, covering pathways, admissions and funding — guidance to help you think it through, not a promise of any particular outcome.
